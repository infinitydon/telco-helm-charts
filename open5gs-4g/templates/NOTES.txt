Open5GS 4G EPC lab installed with ten Multus networks.

Check LTE attach:
  kubectl logs deploy/{{ include "open5gs-4g.fullname" . }}-simulator -c ue

Ping the SGi endpoint through the LTE UE:
  kubectl exec deploy/{{ include "open5gs-4g.fullname" . }}-simulator -c ue -- ping -I tun_srsue {{ .Values.addresses.dataSgi }}
